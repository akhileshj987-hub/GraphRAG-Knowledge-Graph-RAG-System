Reliance Industries Limited was founded by Dhirubhai Ambani in 1966. 
It started as a small textile company in Mumbai and grew into India's largest conglomerate.
Mukesh Ambani, son of Dhirubhai Ambani, became the chairman and managing director of Reliance.
Reliance Jio, a telecommunications subsidiary, was launched in 2016 and revolutionized India's digital landscape.
Nita Ambani is married to Mukesh Ambani and is involved in philanthropy through the Reliance Foundation.
